        Good to know

        The questions planners ask.

        Anything else? Call (786) 660-1341.

        How long do we have the space?

        Private events include 2.5 hours of party time. Want longer? We can do that, but extra time may come with additional costs or a higher minimum.

        Is there a bottle service requirement?

        After 10PM on Thursday, Friday and Saturday, there's a minimum of $450 in bottle service (hard liquor or champagne) for every 4–5 guests. Your total adds it automatically when your party runs past 10PM on those nights. Mixers, juices, sodas, ice and sparklers are included.

        When do I need to lock in my guest count?

        Three business days before the event. If the count goes up, we'll adjust where space allows. If it drops, you're still responsible for the confirmed number.

        Can we split the check?

        Large groups are on a single check, but you can use multiple cards to pay it.

        Can I bring a cake, alcohol or my own playlist?

        No outside cakes, alcohol or playlists. Every menu includes dessert, and we're happy to add a candle for a birthday.

        Are minors allowed?

        Yes, with limits. The latest reservation that includes minors is 7:30PM, and minors may stay until 10:30PM, even with a parent or guardian. Highchairs are free. There's no kids' menu.

        Is there a dress code?

        Business casual, and it's enforced. No beachwear, ripped clothing, beach sandals, or overly casual or transparent outfits. Gentlemen: no tank tops, hats, athletic wear or hoodies.

        Parking, access and entrances?

        Valet is $30 per vehicle, and you can cover it for your guests. The main entrance is at 127 NW 23rd St, with a second at 130 NW 24th St. The venue is fully ADA compliant.

        Is the Terrace private?

        No. You get your own section, but you share the Terrace with the rest of the room. For a fully private space, choose the Patio Tree, Inside, the Upper VIP Area, or a buyout.
