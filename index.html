Good to know

The questions planners ask.

Anything else? Call (786) 660-1341.

What's the deal with service fees?

There's a 20% service charge and 8% sales tax. Both are already in your quote above.

How long do we have the space?

Private events include 2.5 hours of party time. Want longer? We can do that, but extra time may come with additional costs or a higher minimum. After 10PM on Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays, extending your table means adding bottle service.

Is there a bottle service requirement?

After 10PM on Thursday, Friday and Saturday, there's a minimum of $450 in bottle service (hard liquor or champagne) for every 4–5 guests. Bottle service includes mixers, juices, sodas and ice, plus sparklers if you want them.

When do the shows happen?

Show days vary, but performances typically take place on Friday and Saturday nights, starting after 9PM.

When do I need to lock in my guest count?

Three business days before the event. If the count goes up, we'll adjust where space allows. If it drops, you're still responsible for the confirmed number.

Can we split the check?

Large groups are on a single check, but you can use multiple cards to pay it.

Can I bring a cake, alcohol or my own playlist?

No outside cakes, alcohol or playlists. Our prix fixe menus include dessert, and we're happy to add a candle for a birthday. Music is part of the Mayami experience.

Are minors allowed?

Yes, with limits. The latest reservation that includes minors is 7:30PM, and minors may stay until 10:30PM, even with a parent or guardian. Highchairs are available at no charge. There's no kids' menu, but several dishes work well for children.

Is there a dress code?

Business casual, and it's enforced. No beachwear, ripped clothing, beach-style sandals, overly casual or transparent outfits. Gentlemen: no tank tops, hats, athletic wear or hoodies. Worth mentioning in your invite.

Parking, access and entrances?

Valet is $30 per vehicle, and you can cover it for your guests. The main entrance is at 127 NW 23rd St, with a second entrance at 130 NW 24th St. The venue is fully ADA compliant. Just tell your coordinator if any guests need assistance.

Is the Terrace private?

No. You get your own section, but you share the Terrace (and the energy) with the rest of the room. For a fully private space, choose the Patio Tree, Upper VIP Area, Inside, or a buyout.
